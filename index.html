How lending works

You lend Tyr Capital a fixed amount. We owe it back with fixed interest on a fixed schedule. You are a lender, not an investor: you do not share in trading profits or losses.

Term
6 to 24 months
Interest
Fixed rate, simple interest
Repayment
Monthly schedule
Early exit
30 days’ written notice

  1. Inquire

    Send the short form or book a call. We reply within 24 hours by the contact method you choose.

  2. Review the terms

    We walk you through the rate, term and payment schedule and answer your questions. If there is a mutual fit, you receive a formal term sheet.

  3. Sign and fund

    You sign a loan agreement, ideally after your own attorney or advisor has read it. Interest starts the day your funds arrive.

What the agreement gives you

  • A fixed rate for the life of the loan. Simple interest, with no profit share in either direction.
  • A monthly payment schedule. An amortization table shows every payment before you sign.
  • Repayment that does not depend on results. We owe the same payments in a good year or a bad one.
  • A demand right. You can call your full balance at any time with 30 days’ written notice.

The risks, stated plainly

  • The loan is unsecured. No collateral backs it, and you would be a general creditor.
  • It is not a bank deposit. It is not insured by the FDIC or anyone else.
  • Repayment depends on our financial condition. If Tyr Capital became insolvent, you could lose some or all of what you lent.
  • Lend only what you can afford to have at risk. The signed agreement, not this website, sets the terms.

About Tyr Capital

Tyr Capital is a proprietary digital asset trading firm built on the principles of disciplined execution, structured risk management, and deep market research. We do not gamble on volatility; we systematically extract value from it.

The firm is named for the Norse god Tyr, the deity of law, justice, and strategic warfare, and it operates with a precision-first mindset. In an industry often driven by emotion and hype, Tyr Capital relies on data, consistency, and long-term strategic thinking.

Strategy

Tyr Capital employs a structured, rules-based approach to digital asset trading. It combines multi-timeframe technical analysis, market structure mapping, and macro cycle awareness to identify high-probability opportunities across Bitcoin and select altcoin markets.

Every position is governed by defined risk parameters. Capital preservation is the foundation, not a secondary concern. Disciplined entry criteria, pre-determined invalidation levels, and consistent position sizing ensure that no single trade can compromise the broader portfolio.

Research

Tyr Capital publishes independent market research and analysis for traders navigating the digital asset landscape. Coverage spans Bitcoin cycle analysis, macro liquidity conditions, on-chain data interpretation, and emerging developments across blockchain infrastructure.
