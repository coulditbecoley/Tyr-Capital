Risk disclosures

Last updated November 27, 2026

This page describes the main risks of lending money to Tyr Capital LLC. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice and not part of any agreement. If you lend to us, the signed loan agreement is the complete and only statement of your rights and ours. Please have your own attorney, accountant or financial advisor review it before you sign.

You are a lender, not an investor

A loan to Tyr Capital creates a debtor and creditor relationship. You receive no ownership, membership interest or vote, and you do not share in the company's profits or losses. Your fixed interest rate is your entire return. It does not rise if we do well.

The loan is unsecured

No collateral backs the loan. If Tyr Capital could not pay its debts, you would be a general creditor with no claim on any specific asset.

It is not a bank deposit and is not insured

A loan to Tyr Capital is not a deposit. It is not insured by the FDIC or by any other government agency or private insurer.

You could lose some or all of your money

Our obligation to repay you does not depend on trading results. Our ability to repay does depend on the company's financial condition. Tyr Capital trades digital assets, which are volatile, and trading losses could impair that condition. If the company became insolvent, you could lose some or all of the amount you lent. Lend only money you can afford to have at risk.

How loan proceeds are used

Loan proceeds are a general debt of the company. They may be used for any lawful business purpose at our discretion and are not tied to a specific trade or account.

Limited liquidity and transfer

A loan cannot be resold or transferred except as the loan agreement allows. The agreement gives you the right to demand your remaining balance with 30 days' written notice, but that right is only as good as the company's ability to pay when you use it.

Early repayment

We may repay a loan early without penalty. If we do, interest stops on the amount repaid, so you would earn less total interest than the full schedule shows.

No regulatory review

Loans are not registered with or reviewed by any securities or banking regulator. No regulator has passed on the merits of lending to Tyr Capital.

Taxes

Interest you receive may be taxable. We do not give tax advice. Please speak with your own tax advisor.

This website is not the agreement

Nothing on this website is an offer, a promise of any return or a term of any loan. Terms are set only in a signed loan agreement. If anything on this site and a signed agreement differ, the agreement controls.
