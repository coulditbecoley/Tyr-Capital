Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Tyr Capital LLC collects through tyrcapital.io, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you send the lender inquiry form, we receive your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, your preferred loan term, amount range and contact method, and any message you write. The form is processed for us by Formspree.

When you book a call, the booking is handled by Cal.com, which collects your name, email address and anything you add to the booking.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and actions like clicks and scroll depth, and it uses cookies to do so. Fonts on this site are served by Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google when a page loads.

How we use information

We use what you send us to reply to your inquiry, schedule and hold calls, and, if you become a lender, to administer the loan. We use analytics to improve the site. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers named above, which process it on our behalf, with our professional advisors when needed, and where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and loan records for as long as we need them for the relationship or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete an inquiry at any time.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser or use Google's analytics opt-out add-on. You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to tyrcapitalllc@gmail.com.

Children

This site is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to tyrcapitalllc@gmail.com.
